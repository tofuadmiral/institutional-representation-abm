# Phase 1: Institutional Differentiation Results

## Overview

This document summarizes the results from the institutional comparison framework that systematically tested parliamentary and republican systems across four different scenarios.

## Key Findings

### Overall Performance
- **Parliamentary Systems**: 63.7% average passage rate
- **Republican Systems**: 42.8% average passage rate  
- **Efficiency Difference**: +21.0 percentage points for parliamentary

### System Characteristics Confirmed

**Parliamentary Systems:**
- High efficiency through government agenda control
- Strong party discipline (80% vs 40%)
- Vulnerable to fragmentation and coalition instability
- Lower committee gatekeeping (20% kill rate)

**Republican Systems:**
- Deliberative friction through separation of powers  
- Weaker party discipline enables cross-party cooperation
- More robust to political fragmentation
- Higher committee gatekeeping (42% kill rate)

## Scenario-Specific Results

### Baseline Scenario (Balanced)
- **Parliamentary**: 80% passage rate
- **Republican**: 36% passage rate
- **Finding**: Parliamentary dominance in normal conditions

### Fragmented Scenario (5 parties) - **Most Revealing**
- **Parliamentary**: 0% passage rate ⚠️
- **Republican**: 57% passage rate ✅
- **Finding**: Parliamentary systems can become completely paralyzed when highly fragmented, while republican systems remain functional

### Polarized Scenario (Extreme bills)
- **Parliamentary**: 95% passage rate  
- **Republican**: 25% passage rate
- **Finding**: Parliamentary efficiency peaks under strong government control

### Small System Scenario (Minimal viable)
- **Parliamentary**: 80% passage rate
- **Republican**: 53% passage rate
- **Finding**: Scale advantages for parliamentary systems

## Critical Insights

### 1. **Fragmentation Vulnerability**
The fragmented scenario revealed a crucial weakness: parliamentary systems with 5 parties experienced complete governmental breakdown (0 coalition size, 6 confidence vote failures), while republican systems continued functioning through committee-based governance.

### 2. **Institutional Trade-offs**
- **Efficiency vs Robustness**: Parliamentary systems optimize for speed and coherence but risk paralysis
- **Stability vs Representation**: Republican systems maintain functionality across diverse political landscapes at the cost of efficiency

### 3. **Committee Systems**
Both systems use committees, but differently:
- **Parliamentary**: Committees as government tools (20% kill rate)
- **Republican**: Committees as independent gatekeepers (42% kill rate)

## Real-World Implications

These findings validate known comparative politics insights:

1. **Parliamentary** systems work best with:
   - Clear majorities or stable coalitions
   - Strong party discipline
   - Homogeneous political landscapes

2. **Republican** systems excel when:
   - Political landscape is fragmented
   - Cross-party cooperation is needed  
   - Deliberation and checks are valued over speed
