# Paper 3: Vetoes and missed agreements in language-model bargaining

We study what happens when three AI representatives bargain for constituencies with different interests. Earlier experiments examined replacement, handover and peer advocacy. An initial advocacy effect did not survive a matched follow-up. The [working paper](manuscript/main.pdf) follows a different question that arose in the voting experiments. When a veto protects a party from a loss, which offers could still get everyone to agree?

Across 60 paired Qwen games, the party that stood to lose had already asked for a mutually beneficial deal in every game. Showing the proposer how many parties gained from each option increased enacted mutual gains from 6 to 31. Voters saw the same interface in both conditions.
